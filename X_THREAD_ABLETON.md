**Post 1 (hook)**

Don't generate. Buy. 🎛️

Preset tweaking is the biggest time-sink in music production. The producer
who needs a bass patch shouldn't spend 2 hours morphing a Serum preset —
they should buy the finished one and keep making music.

SoundHub is that marketplace. And now it lives inside Ableton Live.

**Post 2 (the problem)**

Buying sound today is chaos:

• loops arrive in DMs, packs on Gumroad, one-shots in Telegram groups
• no provenance — who made it, what's inside, is it licensed?
• credits and splits handled in spreadsheets, paid late or never

Code got repos, attribution and licensing decades ago. Sound is still sold
like loose files in random chats.

**Post 3 (the product)**

SoundHub is a tokenized marketplace for finished, verified sounds:

• presets, loops, stems, packs — DAW-parsed, so you know what's inside
  ("24 Serum presets, 128 BPM, verified") before you pay
• every purchase goes through escrow — no trust-me selling
• 4 license tiers bound to the purchase: Personal, Commercial, Sync,
  Exclusive
• creators get paid automatically when the buyer confirms

**Post 4 (the integration)**

The marketplace stops being a website. It becomes a panel in your DAW.

Open Ableton → SoundHub panel shows:
• the catalog, your purchases, new drops
• suggestions matched to YOUR project — it reads the Live set BPM
  (128 BPM techno → bass presets for 128 BPM techno)

Buy & Load. One click. The preset lands in your project.

**Post 5 (how it works)**

Max for Live device, JS inside Live:

• catalog read straight from the chain (public RPC — no signup to browse)
• context-aware recommendations from the DAW engine
• asset delivered to your library with a short-lived signed link
• web3 stays invisible: no approve, no gas, no RPC — just *buy*
